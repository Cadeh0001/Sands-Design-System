Method

Four Things Drive Your Value

Value = NOI ÷ cap rate. The lease and the real estate decide both.

01 — Income
In-Place NOI
The contract rent, net of any landlord obligations. We verify it against the lease and every amendment — buyers will.
Escalation timing matters: a bump inside the next 12 months is priced; one in year 4 is discounted.
02 — Security
Lease & Guarantor
Remaining term, structure, options, and who stands behind the rent. Corporate paper prices 70–90 bps inside franchisee paper.
Dealer financials, where available, let buyers underwrite coverage — comfort above ~2.0x EBITDAR keeps pricing on the real estate.
03 — Dirt
Real Estate & Location
The land, the corridor, and the trade area. Strong dirt underwrites the downside — what the site is worth if the tenant ever leaves.
Infill sites with high traffic counts and residual land value price measurably inside commodity locations.
04 — Pricing
Cap Rate
Set by the market — but discovered through process. Competitive tension across buyer pools is worth real basis points.
Every 25 bps of compression is roughly 4% of value. The sensitivity grid follows.
