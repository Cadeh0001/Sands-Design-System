The Buy Box Comes First

Six criteria define what we chase — sourced on-market and off-market simultaneously from the day we engage.

Asset Class
Net lease retail, industrial, medical, or specialty — usually spread across two or three sectors.
Size & Count
One large asset or several smaller ones. Smaller diversifies; each adds a closing.
Yield
The going-in cap you are solving for, and how much of it you trade for credit quality.
Lease Terms
Remaining term, NNN vs. NN, escalations, options. Term drives resale value most.
Tenant Credit
Corporate, franchisee, or private — each a different yield and sleep-at-night profile.
Leverage
All cash or financed — replacement debt must match retired debt or the gap is boot.
