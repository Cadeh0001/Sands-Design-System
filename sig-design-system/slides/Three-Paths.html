Three Paths for Your Asset

Sell & 1031 Exchange Sell Outright Continue to Hold
Proceeds Full market value, redeployed tax-deferred into replacement property Full market value, liquid after tax None — equity stays in the dirt
Taxes Gain and recapture deferred in full Gain and depreciation recapture recognized now No event — no diversification either
Income Replaced — often increased — across multiple tenants and markets Ends at closing; proceeds re-invested at will Unchanged — concentrated in one tenant, one roof
Best when Lease has meaningful term left and you want out of single-asset risk You need liquidity, or basis is high and the tax bill is small Rent is far below market and rollover is your upside
Key Takeaway
Remaining lease term is a primary driver of pricing — the same asset is worth measurably different amounts at different points in the lease.
